## 🚨 Slide 2: Problem Statement
1. **1.1 Billion Vulnerable Gig Workers**: Food delivery riders, ride-share drivers, and domestic laborers lack traditional employment benefits or health insurance safety nets.
2. **Exorbitant Premium Thresholds**: Traditional insurance products require high monthly premiums ($50–$200/mo) and multi-week underwriting.
3. **Slow & Opaque Claim Settlement**: Traditional claims take 30–60 days to process with high administrative overhead (15–30% fee margins).
4. **Cross-Border Remittance Friction**: Migrant gig workers sending earnings home lose up to 6.3% in remittance fees.

---

## ⚡ Slide 3: The GigShield Solution
1. **$0.10/day Micro-Contributions**: Workers deposit tiny daily micro-premiums directly into Soroban smart contract pools.
2. **SEP-24 Local Currency Ramps**: Seamless onboarding using local fiat rails (UPI, M-Pesa, GCash, OPay) via regulated Stellar Anchors.
3. **24-Hour Stake-Weighted Peer Governance**: Verified peer validators review evidence (IPFS hashes) and vote on claim validity.
4. **Instant Smart Contract Settlement**: Automated USDC payouts released directly to claimant wallet upon 66% majority vote consensus.
